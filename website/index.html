01 Pairing

Pairing takes one glance.

  1. 1

    Open Pairing on your Mac.

    Trackglass for Mac lives in the menu bar. Choose Pair a device and it shows a code.

  2. 2

    Point your iPad at it.

    The camera reads the code. No typing, no accounts, no passwords. Prefer a 6-digit code? That works too.

  3. 3

    That's it. Forever.

    Both devices keep an encrypted key. Next time they simply find each other, even with no router around.

02 Gestures

Every gesture.
Exactly as you set it.

Trackglass reads your Mac's own Trackpad settings and obeys them, live. Tap to click, secondary click, natural scrolling, tracking speed, three- or four-finger gestures: change them on the Mac, and the glass follows.

  • 1 finger

    Tap to click

    One finger. A haptic tick on the glass tells you it landed.

  • 2 fingers

    Secondary click

    Two-finger tap, or a corner, whichever your Mac is set to.

  • 2 fingers

    Scroll with momentum

    Flick and it coasts. Touch and it stops. Natural or not, your call.

  • 2 fingers

    Pinch to zoom

    Smooth, continuous magnification in Safari, Preview, Maps and more.

  • 2 fingers

    Rotate

    Turn two fingers and the photo turns with them.

  • 3–4 fingers

    Swipe between Spaces

    Three or four fingers sideways, tracking your hand live.

  • 4 fingers

    Mission Control

    Four fingers up to see everything, down for App Exposé.

  • Press & hold

    Force click

    Press and hold for Look Up and Quick Look, with a two-stage haptic.

03 Precision

The same math as macOS.

Pointer acceleration isn't approximated. Trackglass reproduces the parametric acceleration algorithm macOS applies to its own trackpads, in millimeters, at up to 120 frames a second. Slow movements are precise. Fast ones cross the screen.

04 Privacy

Nothing leaves your network.

No account. No cloud. No analytics. Pairing uses a one-time code shown on your Mac, and every session is encrypted with keys only your two devices hold. Peer-to-peer Wi‑Fi means it works even with no router at all.

  • Encrypted on every connection
  • One Mac, one controller at a time
  • Nothing to sign up for, nothing to track
Data pathlocal only

05 Under the hood

Built like a driver, not like a remote.

  • Millimeters in, points out

    Every touch carries its physical position. A 10 mm swipe moves the pointer the same distance on an iPhone and an iPad Pro.

  • Two encrypted channels

    Control over TLS, touch frames over DTLS, both keyed by the pairing that lives only on your devices.

  • Open source, Swift 6

    The engine is a pure Swift package with replayable recordings, so every gesture is a test you can read.
