Help Support

Set up in three steps.

1. Install Trackglass for Mac

  1. Download the latest .dmg from GitHub Releases. It is signed by the developer and notarized by Apple.
  2. Open it and drag Trackglass for Mac to your Applications folder, then launch it. It has no Dock icon: it lives in the menu bar, at the top right of the screen. On a MacBook with a notch, a full menu bar can hide the icon behind the notch; quit or hide a few other menu-bar apps to see it.
  3. When asked, grant Accessibility permission (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → Trackglass for Mac). macOS requires it for any app that moves the pointer.
  4. On macOS 15 or later, also allow Local Network access when macOS asks (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Trackglass for Mac). Without it, your iPad or iPhone cannot find the Mac.

2. Get the iPad / iPhone app

Install Trackglass from the App Store (coming soon). It requires iPadOS or iOS 17 or later. When it first looks for your Mac, iOS asks for Local Network access: allow it.

3. Pair once

  1. On the Mac, click the Trackglass menu bar icon and choose Pair a device…
  2. On the iPad or iPhone, open Trackglass and tap Scan Mac QR code, or tap Use a code instead and type the 6-digit code. (If you scanned the code with the Camera app instead, Trackglass opens and asks you to confirm the pairing.)
  3. On the Mac, click Allow when it asks whether the device may pair.
  4. Done. The devices remember each other. Next time, just open the app.

Common questions

The iPad can't find my Mac. Both devices need to be on the same Wi‑Fi network, with Wi‑Fi turned on. Make sure Trackglass for Mac is running and that Local Network access is allowed on both devices: on the Mac in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network, on the iPad or iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Guest and corporate networks that block traffic between devices may not work.

It's connected but the pointer doesn't move. Accessibility permission is missing or was reset after an update. Open the Trackglass for Mac menu in the menu bar; it shows the permission status and a button that opens the right System Settings pane.

Gestures behave differently from my trackpad. Trackglass follows the Mac's own settings in System Settings → Trackpad (tap to click, secondary click, natural scrolling, tracking speed, More Gestures). Change them there and Trackglass follows within a couple of seconds.

Four-finger gestures don't reach the Mac on iPad. iPadOS uses four- and five-finger gestures for multitasking. Turn them off in Settings → Multitasking & Gestures → Gestures.

I see "Pair again". One side forgot the other (for example after reinstalling). Open Pairing on the Mac and scan again.

Can two iPads control one Mac? One at a time. The second device is told the Mac is busy.

Requirements

  • Mac: macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
  • iPad or iPhone: iPadOS / iOS 17 or later.
