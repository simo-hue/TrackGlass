Support

Set up in three steps.

1. Install Trackglass for Mac

  1. Download the latest .dmg from GitHub Releases. It is signed and notarized by Apple.
  2. Open it and drag Trackglass to your Applications folder, then launch it. It lives in the menu bar.
  3. When asked, grant Accessibility permission (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility). macOS requires it for any app that moves the pointer.

2. Get the iPad / iPhone app

Install Trackglass from the App Store. It requires iPadOS or iOS 17 or later.

3. Pair once

  1. On the Mac, click the Trackglass menu bar icon and choose Pair a device…
  2. On the iPad or iPhone, open Trackglass and scan the code shown on the Mac, or tap Use a code and type the 6-digit code.
  3. Done. The devices remember each other. Next time, just open the app.

Common questions

The iPad can't find my Mac. Both devices need Wi‑Fi turned on. They do not need to be on the same network: Trackglass also connects directly between the two devices (peer-to-peer). On the Mac, make sure the app is running and that Local Network access was allowed when macOS asked. On corporate or guest networks that block device-to-device traffic, move the devices closer together so the peer-to-peer link can take over.

It's connected but the pointer doesn't move. Accessibility permission is missing or was reset after an update. Open the Trackglass menu on the Mac; it shows the permission status and a button that opens the right System Settings pane.

Gestures behave differently from my trackpad. Trackglass follows the Mac's own settings in System Settings → Trackpad (tap to click, secondary click, natural scrolling, tracking speed, More Gestures). Change them there and Trackglass follows within a couple of seconds.

Four-finger gestures don't reach the Mac on iPad. iPadOS uses four- and five-finger gestures for multitasking. Turn them off in Settings → Multitasking & Gestures → Gestures.

I see "Pair again". One side forgot the other (for example after reinstalling). Open Pairing on the Mac and scan again.

Can two iPads control one Mac? One at a time. The second device is told the Mac is busy.

Requirements

  • Mac: macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
  • iPad or iPhone: iPadOS / iOS 17 or later.

Get in touch

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub, or write to SUPPORT_EMAIL. For bugs in gesture recognition, the Diagnostics section in the app's settings can record a short session you can attach to the issue; it contains touch positions only.
