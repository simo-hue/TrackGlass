# Trackglass logo v1

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-22. Raster logo concept; not yet integrated into app asset catalogs. Selected over the earlier glass concepts because their transparent edges contained artifacts.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design a clean, premium app logo for Trackglass, an app that makes an iPhone or iPad a wireless Mac trackpad. Square image, full-bleed solid near-black #0a0a0d background. Center a single simple geometric symbol: a thick continuous rounded rectangle outline suggesting a touch surface, interrupted on its bottom edge where a smooth swipe line curves upward inside the surface and terminates in a circular contact dot. The gesture and touch surface should read as one memorable coherent symbol, with an understated abstract G suggestion. Use icy blue #8fd3ff to soft lavender #c3a6ff for the bold mark. Absolutely flat vector-like clean geometry with a subtle two-color gradient in the mark only; no 3D, no glass textures, no glows, no speckles, no shadows, no text, no letters or wordmark, no extra objects, no Apple logo. Large readable silhouette centered at about 65 percent of canvas width, generous consistent margins. This is a final standalone icon image, not a presentation, not a device mockup, not a grid of variants. Background must be uniform near-black all the way to the square edges.
