### Description [≤ 4000]

```
Trackglass turns your iPhone or iPad into a wireless trackpad for your Mac — one that works like the trackpad built into your MacBook.

Put your phone next to your keyboard, pair it once, and point, click, scroll, pinch and swipe the way you already do. Trackglass reads your Mac's own Trackpad settings, so tap to click, secondary click, natural scrolling, tracking speed, three- and four-finger gestures, Mission Control, App Exposé and Spaces work the way you configured them in System Settings. Hold a finger still to look up the word under the pointer. Change a setting on the Mac and Trackglass follows within seconds.

BUILT FOR PRECISION
• Pointer acceleration that matches macOS, so slow moves are precise and quick flicks cross the screen.
• Touch is sampled at up to 120 Hz on ProMotion displays and delivered over an optimized local link.
• Inertial scrolling, pinch to zoom, rotate, smart zoom and system gestures (they follow your fingers live on macOS 14 to 26).
• Haptic clicks on iPhone, so a tap feels like a click.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Pairing with a QR code or a one-time code, confirmed on your Mac; every session is encrypted.
• Works over your Wi-Fi network: the phone and the Mac just need to be on the same one.
• No account, no analytics, no ads, no data collection. Your touches never leave your network.

MADE TO BE YOURS
• Pure glass surface by default, with themes, accent colors and finger glow you can customize.
• Pair one phone with several Macs and switch with a tap.
• Works on iPhone and iPad, in portrait and landscape.

REQUIRES THE FREE MAC APP
Trackglass needs the free companion app "Trackglass for Mac" (macOS 14 Sonoma or later) running on your Mac. Download it from simo-hue.github.io/TrackGlass (open source). The Mac app asks once for Accessibility permission, which macOS requires to move the pointer, and on macOS 15 or later for Local Network access, so your phone can find it.

iPhone and iPad are trademarks of Apple Inc. Trackglass is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
```

### Notes for the reviewer [paste verbatim]

```
Trackglass turns an iPhone/iPad into a trackpad for a Mac. It requires our free macOS companion app, which is distributed outside the Mac App Store because it must post pointer events (Accessibility API).

To test:
1. On a Mac running macOS 14 or later, download Trackglass for Mac from the latest GitHub release: https://github.com/simo-hue/TrackGlass/releases/latest (DMG signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple; the project is open source). Drag "Trackglass for Mac" to Applications and open it. Grant Accessibility when the Welcome window asks, and on macOS 15 or later allow Local Network access when macOS asks.
2. The Mac app has no Dock icon; its icon is in the menu bar at the top right. On a MacBook with a notch and a full menu bar the icon can be hidden behind the notch; quit a few other menu bar apps if you do not see it. Click it and choose "Pair a device…". A QR code appears.
3. Put the iPhone/iPad on the same Wi-Fi network as the Mac. Open Trackglass, go through onboarding, allow Local Network access when iOS asks, and tap "Scan Mac QR code" (or tap "Use a code instead" and type the 6-digit code shown on the Mac).
4. On the Mac, click "Allow" to accept the pairing request.
5. The phone screen becomes the trackpad: one finger moves the pointer, tap clicks, two fingers scroll, pinch zooms, three/four-finger swipes follow the Mac's Trackpad settings.

Without a Mac, the app still shows onboarding, pairing UI, settings and the trackpad surface in a "No Mac connected" state.

The attached video shows the full flow. Privacy: no accounts, no analytics; the only network traffic is the encrypted local link to the paired Mac.
```
