### Description [≤ 4000]

```
Trackglass turns your iPhone or iPad into a wireless trackpad for your Mac — one that behaves like the trackpad built into your MacBook.

Put your phone next to your keyboard, pair it once, and point, click, scroll, pinch and swipe exactly the way you already do. Trackglass reads your Mac's own Trackpad settings, so tap to click, secondary click, natural scrolling, tracking speed, three- and four-finger gestures, Mission Control, App Exposé, Spaces and Force click all work the way you configured them in System Settings. Change a setting on the Mac and Trackglass follows within seconds.

BUILT FOR PRECISION
• Pointer acceleration that matches macOS, so slow moves are precise and quick flicks cross the screen.
• Touch is sampled at up to 120 Hz on ProMotion displays and delivered over an optimized local link.
• Inertial scrolling, pinch to zoom, rotate, smart zoom and system gestures with live tracking.
• Haptic clicks on your phone, so a tap feels like a click.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Pairing with a QR code or a one-time code; every session is encrypted.
• Works over your Wi-Fi network or directly between the two devices with no router at all.
• No account, no analytics, no ads, no data collection. Nothing leaves your network.

MADE TO BE YOURS
• Pure glass surface by default, with themes, accent colors and finger glow you can customize.
• Pair one phone with several Macs and switch with a tap.
• Works on iPhone and iPad, in any orientation.

REQUIRES THE FREE MAC APP
Trackglass needs the free companion app "Trackglass for Mac" (macOS 14 Sonoma or later) running on your Mac. Download it from simo-hue.github.io/TrackGlass (open source). The Mac app asks for Accessibility permission once, which macOS requires to move the pointer.

iPhone and iPad are trademarks of Apple Inc. Trackglass is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
```

### Notes for the reviewer [paste verbatim]

```
Trackglass turns an iPhone/iPad into a trackpad for a Mac. It requires our free macOS companion app, which is distributed outside the Mac App Store because it must post pointer events (Accessibility API).

To test:
1. On a Mac running macOS 14 or later, download Trackglass for Mac from the latest GitHub release: https://github.com/simo-hue/TrackGlass/releases/latest (notarized DMG, Developer ID signed; the project is open source). Open it, grant Accessibility when prompted.
2. In the Mac's menu bar icon choose "Pair a device…". A QR code appears.
3. Open Trackglass on the iPhone/iPad, go through onboarding, scan the QR code (or tap "Use a code" and type the 6-digit code shown on the Mac). Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network, or close to each other (peer-to-peer Wi-Fi works with no network).
4. The phone screen becomes the trackpad: one finger moves the pointer, tap clicks, two fingers scroll, pinch zooms, three/four-finger swipes follow the Mac's Trackpad settings.

Without a Mac, the app still shows onboarding, pairing UI, settings and the trackpad surface in a "No Mac connected" state.

The attached video shows the full flow. Privacy: no accounts, no analytics; the only network traffic is the encrypted local link to the paired Mac.
```
