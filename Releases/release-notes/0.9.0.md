# Trackglass for Mac 0.9.0 (pre-release)

First public pre-release of the Mac companion for the Trackglass iPhone/iPad app.

- Pointer, clicks, drags and drag lock, two-finger scrolling with momentum, pinch, rotate, smart zoom, three- and four-finger system gestures, Notification Center and force click, mirroring your Mac's own Trackpad settings.
- Pairing by QR code or six-digit code, confirmed with an Allow click on the Mac; the long-term key is agreed with a fresh key exchange and never sent over the network. Every connection is encrypted and stays on your local network.
- Requires macOS 14 or later and the Accessibility permission (the Welcome window walks you through it).

Install: open the DMG and drag Trackglass for Mac to Applications. Updates are delivered in-app.
